# WFRP 4e - Money and Banking Tool

WFRP 4e - Money and Banking Tool

<br>
<br>
Provides a visual interface for sending payment requests and credits to players, including splitting rewards.
<br>
<br>

Automates cost of living calculations based on social status and allows the GM to request payment from the selected actors at the click of a button.

Provides tracking of money banked, and allows the GM to add interest payments with one click (and the option to wipe the whole balance when the players make a bad investment!)

Maintains a bank transaction log for each actor.


# How to use
Open the tool from the Token controls. Select one or more actors on the canvas. This will populate the target list in the tool, and from there you can untick any actors from the selected group that you do not want affected.

<img width="926" height="330" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2823061e-9794-4ca9-80c6-ef059af7a343" />
<br>
<br>
Choose the desired action from the row of buttons below. The tool will give you a preview of the actors’ finances after the action is completed.

<img width="698" height="146" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/53e22683-602b-4b71-bf10-c36ff3cace8d" />
<br>
<br>
Click Send to chat / apply bank action to proceed with the chosen transaction. Payment and credit cards must then be clicked in chat to complete the transaction.

**Install by manifest URL:**

```
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Hendar23/WFRP-Currency-Tool/main/wfrp4e-currency-toolbox/module.json
```

Paste the URL into Foundry's **Install Module** dialog, then enable **WFRP 4e - Money and Banking Tool** in your world. The module appears as a coins button under the Token controls for the GM.
